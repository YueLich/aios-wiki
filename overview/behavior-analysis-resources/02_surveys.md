# 综述论文（Survey / Review）

1. **Mining smartphone data for app usage prediction and recommendations: A survey** — Hong Cao、Miao Lin（A*STAR I2R）— 2017, Pervasive and Mobile Computing — https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pmcj.2017.01.007 — 该领域早期综述。
2. **Smartphone App Usage Analysis: Datasets, Methods, and Applications** — Tong Li、Xia Tong、Huandong Wang（Helsinki + HKUST）— 2022, IEEE COMST — https://doi.org/10.1109/COMST.2022.3163176 — 较新的数据集/方法/应用综述。
3. **A Systematic Survey on Android API Usage for Data-Driven Analytics with Smartphones** — Hansoo Lee、Joonyoung Park、Uichin Lee（KAIST）— 2022, ACM Computing Surveys — https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.11271 — 系统梳理 Android Usage Statistics / Accessibility Service API 采集应用使用/交互数据，OS 级行为日志采集最直接对应综述。
4. **Lifelogging As An Extreme Form of Personal Information Management** — Ly-Duyen Tran、Cathal Gurrin、Alan F. Smeaton（DCU）— 2023, IEEE Data Engineering Bulletin — https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.05767 — lifelogging 领域权威团队总览。
5. **The State-of-the-Art in Lifelog Retrieval: A Review of Progress at the ACM Lifelog Search Challenge 2022–24** — Allie Tran、Cathal Gurrin 等 — 2025, IEEE Access — https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.06743 — 综述个人日志（LLM + 多模态）检索系统前沿。
6. **A Survey on Federated Recommendation Systems** — Zehua Sun、Yonghui Xu、Fangzhao Wu 等（中科院/清华）— 2023, IEEE TNNLS — https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.00767 — 系统综述联邦推荐隐私机制、鲁棒聚合、异构与通信成本。
7. **A Systematic Survey on Federated Sequential Recommendation** — Yichen Li、Qiyu Qin、Ruixuan Li 等（华中科大）— 2025, arXiv — https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.05313 — 序列化用户行为在联邦设定下的综述。
8. **Survey of Federated Learning Models for Spatial-Temporal Mobility Applications** — Yacine Belal、Sonia Ben Mokhtar、Hamed Haddadi 等（Imperial/Huawei）— 2023, arXiv — https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.05257 — 联邦学习用于人类移动、轨迹、位置推荐综述。
9. **A Scenario-Oriented Survey of Federated Recommender Systems** — Yunqi Mi、Xueming Qian 等（西交大）— 2025, arXiv — https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19620 — 从推荐场景角度分析跨设备行为数据联邦化挑战。
10. **A Survey on Point-of-Interest Recommendation: Models, Architectures, and Security** — Qianru Zhang、Hongzhi Yin 等（UQ）— 2025, IEEE TKDE — https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.02191 — 从集中式到联邦/隐私保护的 POI 推荐综述。
11. **360 Quantified Self** — Hamed Haddadi、Ferda Ofli、Ingmar Weber、Jaideep Srivastava（QCRI/Yahoo）— 2015, arXiv — https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.00375 — 整合 EHR/可穿戴/社交数据做全维度个人行为建模的愿景。
